# Raw user stories — angle 4: long-term fun, retention & meta

Generated 2026-08-13 (night design run), Sonnet subagent. Consolidation lives in USER-STORIES.md.

1. **[Designated SoTBookie]** resolving must be one click with context visible — if resolving costs more than creating, the SoTBookie quits and the system dies.
2. **[Unluckiest tank]** stats must show more than "biggest loser" (win rate, streaks, most bets placed) or deep-red net gold makes him decline bets to protect ego.
3. **[Month-2 joiner]** needs visible stake norms (average/suggested stake) to avoid lowballing/overcommitting on bet one.
4. **[Chattiest member]** needs a one-button flavorful result announce, or folklore never gets retold and the addon becomes invisible infrastructure.
5. **[RL who books]** needs quick-bet templates (wipe/no-wipe, first-death, DPS threshold) creatable in <5s between pulls.
6. **[1g min-maxer]** games win% with joke bets — leaderboard needs stake-weighting or gold-based ranking, not raw win%.
7. **[5-loss streak player]** needs soft visibility ("you've lost X this week"), not a hard block — resentment prevention.
8. **[Two rivals]** want head-to-head records ("you vs Thorgar: 9-6") — rivalry needs a scoreboard.
9. **[Officers, progression bets]** bet spans a whole raid night — must survive long open windows and lock/reopen cycles.
10. **[Break fisher]** silly bets must be ceremony-free or variety dies and only "serious" bets remain.
11. **[Returner after 3 raids]** history should read as a digest of what happened, not spreadsheet noise.
12. **[GM]** needs visibility into aging unsettled debts, or drama eventually forces shutting the addon down.
13. **[Big-pot winner]** wants a hall of fame (biggest pot, longest odds) separate from rolling history.
14. **[New tank]** needs discoverability of "what people bet on here" (templates as de-facto greatest hits).
15. **[Dispute suspecter]** resolve must log who resolved and when, visible in history — the paper trail that keeps trust.
16. **[3 concurrent bets on one pull]** UI must separate concurrent bets unambiguously or wrong-camp joins cause arguments.
17. **[Break-even veteran]** fun is participation + variance; don't telegraph "pointlessness."
18. **[SoTBookie during 6-wipe night]** re-open same bet template one click ("Again" button on resolved bet).
19. **[Casual bettor]** no participation-shaming stats; low frequency is fine.
20. **[Old-tier veteran]** leaderboard scope must be defined (season/tier resets vs all-time) or newcomers can't compete.
21. **[Degenerate legend]** derived titles (biggest single win, streaks) — mythology needs names, not just a table.
22. **[Officer, stake inflation]** visibility of stake trends over time; self-regulation before someone gets hurt.

## Bet ideas collected (usable as templates/inspiration)
wipe/no-wipe next pull · who pulls aggro first · tank forgets presence/stance · who dies first · top DPS parse · X DPS broken · kill time over/under · highest roll on a gray item · who gets CC'd · lowest overheal · who releases before combat res · first into fire/off platform · enrage timer beaten · fishing contest at break · duel bracket at invite lull · who DCs first tonight · hunter pet dies first · exact attempts to first kill · who forgot to repair.

## Top 5 retention drivers
1. Visible, evolving folklore — history/stats retold in chat.
2. Low-friction quick-bets/templates.
3. Fun derived stats & titles, not just numbers.
4. Seasonal/tier resets for realistic leaderboard competition.
5. Betting variety beyond wipes (silly non-combat bets).

## Top 5 fun-killers
1. Real debt and resentment from stake creep.
2. Stat-gaming with trivial stakes.
3. Dispute-prone resolution with no paper trail.
4. SoTBookie burnout (high-effort booking every pull).
5. Zero discoverability/norms for new members.
